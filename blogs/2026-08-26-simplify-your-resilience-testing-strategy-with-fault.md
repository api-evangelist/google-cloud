When databases fail and network paths falter, you still need your mission-critical cloud services to stay online. Yet guaranteeing high availability has become increasingly difficult because of the complexity of modern distributed systems. To help you maintain availability and reliability during adverse events, we’re announcing Fault Injection Testing in preview.
