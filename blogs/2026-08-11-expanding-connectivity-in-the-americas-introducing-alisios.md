The global digital economy relies on robust, resilient, and highly secure infrastructure to support everything from daily internet use to telehealth and scientific research breakthroughs. Today, Google is announcing a major expansion of our global network infrastructure in the Americas with three new subsea cable systems — Alisios, Canoa, and OlaLuz. These systems, alongside a new branch for the Firmina cable and previous investments in Curie , Nuvem , and Sol , form Americas Connect .
