To defend against the growing range of AI-accelerated threat actors, organizations need to be able to respond faster to outpace the adversary. Recently, we announced Google AI Threat Defense , an automated security system designed to help you continuously monitor for and stop AI-powered threats before they can impact your business. Based on Google’s own approach to today’s threats and vulnerability management, it’s centered on a four-step framework: Prepare, scan and prioritize, remediate, and monitor.
