Google announced performance enhancements for Managed Spark clusters including the Lightning Engine delivering up to 4.9x faster performance, Flexible VMs for improved resource availability, and zero-scale cluster capabilities for cost optimization. New agentic capabilities integrate with Gemini AI for development workflows, along with scheduled stops for resource management.
