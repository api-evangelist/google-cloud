AlloyDB powers enterprise-grade search for some of the largest organizations, providing robust hybrid search capabilities that combine text, vector, and keyword searches into a simple ranked SQL query. And with our recent launch of RUM index support , AlloyDB customers now have even more powerful full-text search capabilities at their fingertips. However, database developers often face limitations when indexing continuous text in logographical languages like Chinese, Japanese, and Korean, where traditional whitespace-based tokenization fails.
