Can teams of AI agents collaborate to create a short film? As part of an internal Google generative media hackathon, we put this question to the test – specifically, to uncover whether AI agents could work collaboratively in a domain less innately familiar than software development. We gave each crew three agents with distinct roles and had them collaborate through messages and shared files under their own agent-only hackathon.
