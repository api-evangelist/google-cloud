In Google Cloud, Identity and Access Management (IAM) helps you maintain access control over your cloud resources and operations. While it includes other features, this is its primary purpose. If you ever tried to harden security over your application, you know the importance of the Principle of Least Privilege ( PoLP ) ‒ grant the absolute minimum permissions to your users and workloads to allow them to perform their tasks.
