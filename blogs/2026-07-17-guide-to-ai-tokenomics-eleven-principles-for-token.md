Optimizing token consumption is key to keeping AI coding assistants fast and accurate. You might not be writing every line of code any more, but now you’re responsible for directing those coding assistants to focus on getting the most out of each token. Context bloat increases latency and causes models to forget instructions or hallucinate, it also costs money and drives human attention away from the problems that actually matter.
