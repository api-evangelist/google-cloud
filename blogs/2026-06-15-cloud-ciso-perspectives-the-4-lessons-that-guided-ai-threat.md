Google Cloud CISO Chris Betz outlines four lessons from developing AI Threat Defense: Prepare, Scan and prioritize, Remediate, and Monitor. He notes that a team equipped with multiple AI models can find thousands of vulnerabilities in hours or minutes versus months previously, and emphasizes a three-R's strategy of Refresh, remove, and rewrite for securing open-source software.
