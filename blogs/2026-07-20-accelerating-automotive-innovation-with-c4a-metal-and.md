As the automotive landscape accelerates toward software-defined vehicles, Cockpit Domain Controllers (CDCs) are becoming the core of next-generation in-cabin experiences. The ability to rapidly develop, test, and validate CDC software in a flexible, hardware-independent environment is critical for innovation and time-to-market. However, physical hardware constraints and the requirement for high-performance graphics present significant challenges for global development teams.
