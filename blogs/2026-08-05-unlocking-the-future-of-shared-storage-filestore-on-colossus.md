Today, enterprise storage must be as agile, elastic, and responsive as the workloads it supports. Filestore, Google Cloud’s first-party, secure, scalable NFS file service, can service a wide-range of enterprise use cases as well as cutting-edge AI and agentic workflows. Today, we’re sharing a major enhancement: Filestore now incorporates a cloud-native backend storage layer built directly on Colossus , Google’s foundational distributed storage system.
