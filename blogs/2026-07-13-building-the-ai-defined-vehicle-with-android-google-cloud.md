The automotive industry is moving from building hardware-centric platforms toward building their own sophisticated Software-Defined Vehicle (SDV) architectures. For OEMs, a vehicle is no longer just a way to go from point A to point B, but an intelligent, connected node within an AI-native ecosystem! With its partners, Google’s Android and Google Cloud are at the forefront of this transition.
